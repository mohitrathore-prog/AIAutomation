Governing Engineering & Transformation Principles

Non-negotiable architectural rules designed to protect capital and eliminate unnecessary technology complexity.

Principle 1
Business Problem First
Always start with: "What is the business problem?" Never ask: "Where can we use AI?" Technology serves the business outcome.
Principle 2
AI is NOT the Default
If a rule, deterministic workflow, standard API, or script solves the problem, AI is explicitly rejected. Rules have 100% auditability and zero token cost.
Principle 3
Existing Technology First
Before recommending new software, evaluate whether existing SAP, ServiceNow, Microsoft 365, or Salesforce systems already possess native capabilities.
Principle 4
Economic Justification
AI must demonstrate sufficient incremental financial value over simpler alternatives. If incremental ROI is inadequate: "AI applicable but economically unjustified."
